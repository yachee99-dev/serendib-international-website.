THE PURPOSE OF SAMPLING

Not just a sample.
A better-informed sourcing decision.

Where feasible, we coordinate product-matched sampling and the supporting questions buyers need answered before a bulk order. Sampling is a separately quoted service, not an automatic free offer.

01

Specification-led

Start with product type, grade, format, application, destination and intended commercial volume.

02

Supplier-matched

We assess potential sources and ask them to confirm sample availability, documentation and preparation times.

03

Transparent costs

Any product sample charge, agreed coordination fee and freight are listed separately before acceptance.

04

Next-step clarity

Sample evaluation can inform the subsequent bulk quotation, packaging discussion and compliance review.

THE SAMPLING JOURNEY

A straightforward process.
No surprises.

Our objective is to reduce uncertainty for international buyers while keeping every commercial commitment grounded in supplier confirmation.

01

Submit your brief

Tell us product, specifications, target volume, destination and sample expectations.

02

Feasibility assessment

We seek supplier confirmation of availability, suitable grade and any relevant documents.

03

Written sample offer

Confirm itemized costs, delivery responsibilities, estimated timing and payment terms.

04

Buyer approval

Nothing is ordered or shipped before agreement. Sampling proceeds only once payment and supplier terms are secured.

COMMERCIAL CLARITY

What a quotation may include.

Product sample: material, grade and any preparation or packing required.

Sourcing and coordination: an agreed service fee, when applicable.

Courier and customs: freight, documentation and destination charges where relevant.

Bulk order pathway: next-step sourcing options and any potential sample credit, only if separately agreed in writing.

We agree the sample specification, availability, estimated timing and any courier charges in a written quotation before payment. Share the import documents or laboratory tests your market requires so we can review those with the supplier.
