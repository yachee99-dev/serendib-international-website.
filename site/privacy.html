Enquiry privacy

Serendib Products Pvt Ltd trades as Serendib International.

Email drafts

The worksheet prepares a brief in your browser. If you choose to email it, your email provider sends the details you include to the sourcing desk. The desk uses them to assess your request and respond.

Online enquiries

Online submission is disabled in this staging build. If enabled after deployment review, Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated traffic. Your approved submission would be recorded for sourcing review and sent via the configured email delivery service to our company inbox. Avoid including payment details or sensitive personal information.

Product photography

Catalogue images are generated product references. Product appearance and final specification can be reviewed with a supplier sample.

Website and contact

The staging site uses no analytics or advertising scripts. For questions about the handling of your details, email info@serendibinternational.com.
