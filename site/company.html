ABOUT SERENDIB INTERNATIONAL

Your sourcing partner in Sri Lanka

Our team brings over a decade of experience supplying products to trade partners in the Middle East and Asia. Today, we help international buyers find and assess Sri Lankan supply options.

One point of contact for your buying brief

Serendib International is the trading brand of Serendib Products Pvt Ltd. We work with independent Sri Lankan manufacturers and suppliers across spices, coconut ingredients, cocopeat and tea. You share what your market needs, and we coordinate the supplier conversations, samples and export requirements.
