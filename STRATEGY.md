# Serendib V9 creative direction

## Why V8.1 drifts

The first screen devoted more weight to an interactive brief than to the company. Buyers encountered route radios and specification hints before seeing why the company is useful. Imagery sat inside the selector, at thumbnail scale. The warm palette softened the interface but left its information hierarchy intact. The introduction had no product-led buying story, and the commercial benefit of a single relationship appeared late on the page.

## Benchmarks

- [Dilmah](https://www.dilmahtea.com/ranges) gives distinct teas distinct stories and leads with product character. Serendib can use origin and form as editorial material while keeping manufacturing and ownership claims out of its narrative.
- [ofi](https://shop.ofi.com/spice-facts) makes origins, product forms and processing tangible for ingredient buyers. Use relevant purchasing distinctions and preserve product-specific limitations.
- [Nedspice](https://www.nedspice.com/traceability/) demonstrates its promise through a concrete record of what can be traced. Serendib can show what it will help the buyer confirm and let later supplier-specific evidence substantiate the details.
- [CeySpice](https://ceyspice.com/quality/) explains product checks and procurement implications. A dated specification and a documented sample pathway are stronger future trust assets than decorative seals.
- [Sri Lanka Export Development Board](https://www.srilankabusiness.com/spices/) provides origin context for the spice collection. Serendib does not borrow marks or imply certification.

## Three directions

| Direction | Business credibility | Originality and buyer appeal | Feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin journal | Strong product atmosphere. Commercial service can feel secondary. | Rich photographic chapters invite exploration, with a risk of reading like a travel editorial. | Needs authentic origin assignments and documentary permissions to sustain multiple stories. |
| Trading-house editorial | Opens with a personal commercial relationship at origin. Product imagery and buying knowledge substantiate the proposition. | Strong contrast between charcoal trade stationery, a full-scale still life and warm neutral product plates. Focused and relevant to business buyers. | Strong with current product imagery and established role. Room to add authentic samples and docs when available. |
| Private-client briefing room | Direct, tailored and process-oriented. | Feels useful to qualified prospects but asks first-time visitors to learn an interface. | Existing RFQ technology supports it today. Better as an optional destination. |

Selected trading-house editorial. Use compact typography, deeply colored stationery, panels that follow the narrative and responsive photography. Put the company and its point of view ahead of SKU discovery. Put the discovery tool at its own URL. Keep buyers one action away from contact or a quote. This direction uses imagery of product types and the established experience of the team. It requires no supplier-level claims.
